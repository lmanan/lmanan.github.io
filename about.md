I graduated in mechanical engineering from [IIT Kanpur](https://www.iitk.ac.in/) and then completed a masters in applied mechanics from the [Chalmers University](https://www.chalmers.se/en/Pages/default.aspx). From 2014 up till 2017, I was working in the field of acoustics in the automotive industry. During this period, I was writing scripts in Python and MATLAB, building graphical interfaces and playing with differential equations. 

Currently I use computer vision methods to analyse volumetric imaging data in the context of early development of the *Platynereis* worm. I am excited about future progresses where it would be possible to integrate multi-modal data (say combining computer-vision datasets such as microscopy time-lapse acquisitions and NLP datasets such as single cell RNA sequencing data to infer gene regulatory networks; or combining cryo electron microscopy images and NLP-like protein sequences to infer the underlying 3D protein structures). 

Thank you for stopping by.
